A MAN bus from Breda to the port of Antwerp

Too high for an ordinary semi-trailer, ready for Ghana

A customer bought a MAN Lion’s Regio in Breda: a long, high bus with 50 to 60 seats. Destination: Tema, the largest port of Ghana. First it still had to get to the port of Antwerp.

Driving was not possible, and on an ordinary semi-trailer the bus came out above 4 metres. Bridges over the motorway are usually right at that height. Together with our regular partner we drove with a low loader, a trailer with an extra-low loading floor, so that bus and trailer together could pass under the bridges.

The bus arrived safely in the port of Antwerp, ready to be shipped. It did not stop there. We asked several shippers for a price. The customer wanted to stay with his regular ship broker, but with those quotes in hand he got a lower price from his own broker.

Towing a 4-metre-high bus does not take strength, but a plan: the right trailer, the right route and papers that are in order.

Broken down on the motorway? This is how it works with FAST

On the Flemish motorways, and also on the hard shoulder and the car parks along the motorway, a fixed arrangement applies. A vehicle that can no longer drive is removed there by the FAST towing service that the Flemish government has designated for that stretch of motorway. This also applies to trucks. So at that moment you do not choose yourself who comes.

Where we do help: afterwards. The FAST towing service takes your truck safely off the motorway, usually to their depot. From there Faster can collect it and take it to your own garage, the brand dealer or your home base, even if that is in the Netherlands, France or further. If your truck is on an ordinary road, on business premises or in a car park off the motorway, you can call us right away.

Too wide, too high or too heavy: exceptional transport

In Belgium, a transport becomes exceptional as soon as it exceeds the legal dimensions. The limits that most often apply: 2.55 metres wide, 4 metres high and 44 tonnes for a combination with five axles. A loaded excavator or a high coach quickly goes over that.

Then a low loader and a permit are needed. In Flanders that permit comes from the Agentschap Wegen en Verkeer. We arrange the trailer, look at the route and make sure the papers are in order before the vehicle sets off.

What does a truck breakdown service cost?

A fixed price for every truck does not exist, and anyone who promises you one over the phone without asking questions is guessing. What determines the price:

  • the weight and the type: an empty tractor unit, a loaded combination or a bus;
  • where the vehicle is and how accessible it is;
  • the distance to the destination;
  • the time: in the middle of the night or on a Sunday;
  • what needs to happen on site: just towing, loading, or first righting it with a crane.

You get the price in advance. For transport companies with several trucks, we can make fixed arrangements, so that the planner does not have to negotiate first when there is a breakdown.

FAQ

Questions about truck breakdown service

My truck has broken down on the motorway. Can I call you directly then?
On the Flemish motorways, hard shoulders and car parks along the motorway, the designated FAST towing service removes the vehicle. We take over after that: from the depot to your garage, brand dealer, own premises or back abroad.
What does a truck breakdown service cost?
That depends on the weight, the location, the distance, the time and what needs to be done on site. You get the price before we set off.
Do you also help with buses and coaches?
Yes. City buses, service buses and coaches. With passengers on board, we first help think about how the people can continue, then about the bus itself.
Do you also transport machines and site equipment?
Yes: excavators, telehandlers, forklifts, aerial work platforms and other rolling equipment. For what is too wide, too high or too heavy, we arrange a low loader and the permit for exceptional transport.
Can my truck also go back to the Netherlands or another country?
Yes. We take trucks, buses and semi-trailers to the garage or depot you choose, also across the border. You hear the price in advance.
What do I need to pass on when I call?
Your location (live location via WhatsApp is easiest), number plate, make and type, whether the truck is loaded, what happened and where it needs to go. Photos help.
