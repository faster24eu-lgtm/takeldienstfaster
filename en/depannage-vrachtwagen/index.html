Broken down on the motorway? This is how it works with FAST

On the Flemish motorways, and also on the hard shoulder and the car parks along the motorway, a fixed arrangement applies. A vehicle that can no longer drive is removed there by the FAST breakdown recovery that the Flemish government has designated for that stretch of motorway. This also applies to lorries. So at that moment you do not choose yourself who comes.

Where we do help: afterwards. The FAST breakdown recovery takes your lorry safely off the motorway, usually to their depot. From there Faster can collect it and take it to your own garage, the brand dealer or your home base, even if that is in the Netherlands, France or further. If your lorry is on an ordinary road, on business premises or in a car park off the motorway, you can call us right away.

Too wide, too high or too heavy: exceptional transport

In Belgium, a transport becomes exceptional as soon as it exceeds the legal dimensions. The limits that most often apply: 2.55 metres wide, 4 metres high and 44 tonnes for a combination with five axles. A loaded excavator or a high coach quickly goes over that.

Then a low loader and a permit are needed. In Flanders that permit comes from the Agentschap Wegen en Verkeer. We arrange the trailer, look at the route and make sure the papers are in order before the vehicle sets off.

What does a HGV breakdown recovery cost?

A fixed price for every lorry does not exist, and anyone who promises you one over the phone without asking questions is guessing. What determines the price:

  • the weight and the type: an empty tractor unit, a loaded combination or a bus;
  • where the vehicle is and how accessible it is;
  • the distance to the destination;
  • the time: in the middle of the night or on a Sunday;
  • what needs to happen on site: just towing, loading, or first righting it with a crane.

You get the price in advance. For transport companies with several lorries, we can make fixed arrangements, so that the planner does not have to negotiate first when there is a breakdown.

FAQ

Questions about HGV breakdown recovery

My lorry has broken down on the motorway. Can I call you directly then?
On the Flemish motorways, hard shoulders and car parks along the motorway, the designated FAST recovery service removes the vehicle. We take over after that: from the depot to your garage, brand dealer, own premises or back abroad.
What does a HGV breakdown recovery cost?
That depends on the weight, the location, the distance, the time and what needs to be done on site. You get the price before we set off.
Do you also help with buses and coaches?
Yes. City buses, service buses and coaches. With passengers on board, we first help think about how the people can continue, then about the bus itself.
Do you also transport machines and site equipment?
Yes: excavators, telehandlers, forklifts, aerial work platforms and other rolling equipment. For what is too wide, too high or too heavy, we arrange a low loader and the permit for exceptional transport.
Can my lorry also go back to the Netherlands or another country?
Yes. We take lorries, buses and semi-trailers to the garage or depot you choose, also across the border. You hear the price in advance.
What do I need to pass on when I call?
Your location (live location via WhatsApp is easiest), number plate, make and type, whether the lorry is loaded, what happened and where it needs to go. Photos help.
