Not every breakdown calls for the same solution. That is why we always start by listening and then look at what is really needed.

01

Roadside assistance

Flat battery, flat tyre, starting problems, locked out of your vehicle or another problem that stops you from continuing. I first try to understand what has happened and whether you can be helped on the spot.

02

Towing service & recovery

From a classic tow to an accident, fire, heavy damage or a vehicle that has ended up in a difficult place. The approach depends on the situation and the vehicle.

03

Car transport

Transport to a garage, home, the port, a buyer, a seller or another destination. Also when the transport is not urgent.

04

Vans & light freight

Larger vehicles require different capacity and a different loading method. My 7-tonner is equipped for that.

05

Machines & forklifts

Forklifts are not big cars. The weight is distributed differently and loading them incorrectly can cause damage. That is why I work here with the right equipment and an adapted loading strategy.

06

International repatriation

Vehicles from or to France, Spain, Germany, Italy, Austria, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, Ireland and beyond. With experience in ferries, import/export and documentation.

07

Safety during a breakdown

In dangerous places, safety comes before everything. If possible, switch on your hazard lights, put on a high-visibility vest as soon as you leave your vehicle on a motorway/expressway and make sure there is signalling. The current Belgian highway code states that the warning triangle must be placed at least 30 m away on ordinary roads and at least 100 m away on motorways when that signalling is required; do not put yourself in danger to place it.
