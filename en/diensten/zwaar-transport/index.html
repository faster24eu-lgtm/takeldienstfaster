HEAVY & EXCEPTIONAL TRANSPORT

The ‘loading low loader’ for exceptional transport (transport exceptionnel)

Ever heard of a MAN Lion’s Regio bus? No? This is a large, high and long coach made by MAN with 50 to 60 seats, which is often used for public transport.

Bus transport from Breda to …

A customer bought one of these from Abid Trading B.V. in Breda with the aim of… transporting it to Tema, the most important port city of Ghana! But how do you arrange the transport of a bus with such extraordinary dimensions?

Faster became his partner in this story.

The phone rang at Faster. The customer on the other end of the line asked us to arrange truck transport for a bus from Breda to Antwerp. He had an appointment with a shipbroker for shipping the bus to Tema, and so the bus had to be brought to the port of Antwerp. We sprang into action and, together with our regular partner, we took care of the transport. And not just any transport!

Exceptional transport

As indicated above, the dimensions of the bus are not standard and a ‘low loader’ is needed. We can hear you thinking a … what? A ‘low loader’ is the English word for a dieplader. The loading height of the trailer has to be low enough so that the bus can be transported on it. After all, we have to take into account the fact that the bridges over the motorways are generally 4 metres high and so we have to be able to get under them with the loader and the bus. Once above 4 metres and 4.3 metres, you must have a permit. Expertise, knowledge and the right equipment ensured that the bus arrived safely in the port of Antwerp. Ready to be shipped!

A little extra

Our service did not stop here. We were convinced that we could arrange a cheaper shipbroker for our customer. We contacted various companies that shipped vehicles and collected competing quotes. Our customer clearly indicated that his preference was to stay with his trusted shipbroker, but by negotiating with the quotes we had obtained, we were able to lower the price with his existing shipping partner. Extra service!
