SERVICE AREA

I do not know Deurne from a map.

Deurne is close by: usually I am at the edge of Deurne within about 13 minutes, at the exit right next to the Lotto Arena. From there I am quickly with you, wherever you are in Deurne. Most recoveries take place on and around the N120 and the side streets there.

In Deurne I know, among others, the garages along the Ten Eekhovelei and the Bisschoppenhoflaan. On the Melkerijstraat, I work with Old School Mechanics for specialist engine work.

REGULAR PARTNERS

Parts and specialist work.

For parts, I work with Victoria Auto Parts and Recuparts — I sometimes deliver a vehicle or part there, or collect an engine that is too big to transport themselves. On the Melkerijstraat, I work with Old School Mechanics for specialist engine work and with their neighbour AM Tronic for programming on-board computers.
