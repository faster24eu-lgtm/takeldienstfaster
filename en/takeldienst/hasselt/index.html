AT THE SIDE OF THE ROAD?

Safety first, then Faster.

Safety above all! A breakdown with your vehicle is a pain, but think first of your safety, then of the rest.

Safe placeTake yourself and your passengers to a safe place, if possible behind the crash barrier.
Hi-vis vestHave all occupants, including yourself, put on a hi-vis vest. That increases your visibility considerably.
Hazard lights & triangleSwitch on your hazard lights and place (if it is safe to do so) your warning triangle.
Only then callOnly call Faster when everyone is safe. Give your location, situation and vehicle, possibly with a photo via WhatsApp.

Driving on the wrong fuel can damage your vehicle. Call us and we will suggest various solutions.
My car has been towed?
For a car towed after illegal parking, you need to go to the service that carried out the towing. This is often done in cooperation with the city. Ask them where your car is. This is a different procedure from breakdown assistance.

First make sure that you and your passengers are safe. Only then do you call Faster. If you take care of yourself and your passengers, we take care of your vehicle.
