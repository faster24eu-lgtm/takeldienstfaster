AARTSELAAR IN PRACTICE

What is often needed in Aartselaar.

Aartselaar combines a lot of car trade with a quieter residential area. That brings various situations with it.

Cars & vans

From an ordinary breakdown intervention to transport of a van, depending on the vehicle and the situation.

Around the A12

Aartselaar lies on the A12, a busy connecting road between Antwerp and Boom. A breakdown on or right next to this road requires extra attention to safety.

Car trade & dealers

There is a lot of car trade active in Aartselaar. I regularly transport vehicles to and from a dealer or another agreed destination in the area.

Underground car parks

Not every vehicle fits as easily into an underground car park. Depending on the height and the space, I use a smaller, suitable vehicle if necessary.

Local context

In Aartselaar I come, among other places, near Hammertime Auctions, Cardoen, Stellantis Group and Beerens, where vehicles regularly need to be transported to a dealer, the customer’s garage or another destination.

Common situations

I get a wide range of calls: a vehicle that no longer drives, an electrical or mechanical fault, a problem with the clutch or gearbox, or simply transport after a purchase or sale. Tell me what you notice and where the vehicle is, and I will look together with you at what is needed.
