FAQ or common requests for help.

Every situation is unique. We listen to you, analyse the situation and look for the appropriate solution. Some problems are unique, some occur more often.

Common requests for help:

1

Flat tyre

Driving on with a flat tyre is not always possible and can cause damage to your vehicle. Often, direct transport to the tyre centre is the cheapest and most efficient solution. We are happy to help you further!

2

Flat battery and starting problems

Does your car not start? That may be due to your battery. No worries, if it is safe and technically possible, we will get your car started again with a booster. Contact us!

3

Wrong fuel

It can happen to anyone, filling up with the wrong fuel! Driving on can cause a lot of damage to your car. Call us, we offer various solutions.

4

Vehicle towed by the city or police

Administratively towed after illegal parking? For this you need to find out which service carried out the towing and where the vehicle is. This is not roadside assistance.

Safety first, then Faster.

It can be quite busy in and around Antwerp. Have you broken down on the road? Then think first of your safety and that of your passengers. Make sure you are safe first before you call us for help. Switch on your hazard lights. Put on your high-visibility vest. If possible, stand behind the crash barrier and (if it is safe) place your warning triangle. Do not concern yourself with the technical side. We take care of your vehicle. You take care of your safety.

Giving a correct location saves a lot of time.

The Antwerp roads and tunnels hold no secrets for us!

Our experience means that in our home base we know the traffic arteries in and around the city. We also know that it is often very busy and dangerous, so only contact us once you are in a safe place.
