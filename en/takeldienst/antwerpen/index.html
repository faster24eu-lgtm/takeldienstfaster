Read the full story

Did you know that… a tow with a crane is the most expensive form of breakdown assistance there is, because the vehicle actually has to be lifted? Of course nobody is happy at that thought.

They asked for our help and within half an hour we were on site with a brainstorm of smart and cheaper solutions in our heads. Through our years of experience we know that the most expensive solution is not always the fastest and best!

We would like to spare you the technical story of the ‘magic red lever’, but (thanks to our necessary knowledge) we got the car into neutral despite the blocked ignition. And this without needing a crane! From then on the Fiat Ducato could be towed in the classic and cheaper way. We took the van straight to the garage where the ignition lock could be repaired.

FAQ or common requests for help.

Every situation is unique. We listen to you, analyse the situation and look for the appropriate solution. Some problems are unique, some occur more often.

Common requests for help:

Flat tyre

Driving on with a flat tyre is not always possible and can damage your vehicle. Often a direct transport to the tyre centre is the cheapest and most efficient solution. We are happy to help you further!

Flat battery and starting problems

Your car won’t start? That may be down to your battery. No worries, if it is safe and technically possible, we will get your car started again with a booster. Contact us!

Wrong fuel

It can happen to anyone, filling up with the wrong fuel! Driving on can do a lot of damage to your car. Call us, we offer various solutions.

Vehicle towed by city or police

Towed administratively after illegal parking? For this you need to find out which service did the towing and where the vehicle is. This is not a breakdown service.

Safety first, then Faster.

It can get pretty busy in and around Antwerp. Have you broken down on the road? Then think first of your safety and that of your passengers. Make sure you are safe before you call us for help. Switch on your hazard lights. Put on your hi-vis vest. If possible, stand behind the crash barrier and place your warning triangle (if it is safe). Don’t busy yourself with the technical part. We take care of your vehicle. You take care of your safety.

Giving an accurate location saves a lot of time.
