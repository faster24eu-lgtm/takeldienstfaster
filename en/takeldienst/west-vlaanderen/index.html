Broken down on the road?!

Safety first. Then Faster.

Breaking down is above all not fun, but keep a clear head and put your safety before everything.

First take care of the safety of yourself and your passengers. Start with visibility: everyone put on a hi-vis vest and switch on your hazard lights. If it can be done safely, also place your warning triangle.

Are you visible and in a safe place? Then you can contact Faster. Give your exact location (or share it via WhatsApp) and tell us about your situation. You can also send a photo, that can be handy.

No technical knowledge? Not necessary! Tell us what you see on your vehicle and where you are, that is enough for us. We take care of the technical side and the solution.

Common situations

Flat tyre?

Driving with a flat tyre is not safe and can damage your vehicle. Often transport to a tyre centre is the most economical solution. More efficient and cheaper than an extensive intervention on the spot.

Flat battery?

We try to start your car with a booster where that can be done safely. Is this not possible technically or for safety reasons? Then we take your vehicle to the garage.

Wrong fuel?

The wrong fuel can have nasty consequences for your car. Depending on the situation, a technician comes on site or I take your vehicle to a garage.

Owner: “Every situation is different. We analyse the situation and try to offer the most efficient solution possible.”
