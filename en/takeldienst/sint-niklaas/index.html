Towing Service Sint-Niklaas: what would you like to know?

Practical answers for when you have broken down or need a vehicle transported.

Can you be reached 24/7?
Yes. In an urgent situation I answer myself, even at night.
How quickly can you be in Sint-Niklaas?
Sint-Niklaas is about 30 minutes from Antwerp in normal traffic. In an urgent situation I try to set off as quickly as possible; for a planned trip I simply agree a time.
Can you take my car to my own garage?
Yes. You choose where the car goes; I give advice if that is useful.
Do you also transport vans?
Yes, vehicles up to 3,5 tonnes.
Can you help if the car does not start?
Often, yes. I first look at whether the problem can be solved on site, for example with a booster, or whether the vehicle does have to be towed after all.
Do you also carry out planned transports?
Yes. Not every trip is urgent. If the car is in a safe place and only needs to be moved later, I simply schedule it.
Do you also transport vehicles abroad?
Yes. I know the route and work with a network of contacts for international transport.
How does the price work?
I discuss the price in advance. If the information you pass on is correct, I stick to that agreement, with no surprises afterwards.
