COMMON SITUATIONS

Four situations I am often called for in Limburg.

Not every breakdown situation is the same. Just tell me what you notice, and I will look together with you at what is needed.

Flat tyre

Driving with a flat tyre is not always possible or safe. It is often cheaper to take the vehicle directly to a tyre centre instead of an extensive call-out on site.

Flat battery & starting problems

A vehicle that does not start does not have to go straight to the garage. Where it is safe and technically possible, I first try to start it with a booster.

Wrong fuel

Filled up with the wrong fuel? Do not simply drive on. Depending on the situation, I call in a technician or transport the vehicle to a garage.

Vehicle towed by the city or police

Was your vehicle administratively towed for illegal parking? That goes through a different procedure from a breakdown call-out. First find out which service carried out the towing and where the vehicle is. For an ordinary breakdown situation or accident you can call me directly.

URGENT

Are you on the road or in an unsafe situation?

Are you stationary on the carriageway, holding up traffic or unable to leave the vehicle safely yourself? Then it is urgent for you, and therefore for me too.

PLANNED

Is the vehicle already in a safe place?

Does your car simply need to go from home to the garage tomorrow? Then I will schedule that at a time that suits.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Towing Service Limburg: what would you like to know?

Practical answers for when you have broken down or need a vehicle transported.

Can you be reached 24/7 in Limburg?
Yes. Faster can be reached 24/7, also in the evening, at night and at weekends.
How quickly can you be on site?
My crew sets off from Zonhoven, centrally located in the province. In Zonhoven itself you are usually helped within about 10 minutes, in Hasselt within about 20 minutes. In the rest of Limburg it is usually between 15 and 45 minutes, depending on exactly where you are. For the furthest corners of the province I would rather give an honest estimate than a fixed promise.
Do you work throughout Limburg?
Yes. I drive throughout the whole province, from Hasselt and Genk to Lommel, Maaseik, Sint-Truiden, Tongeren and Pelt. If you are somewhere else in Limburg, I will look into it on request.
How does the price work?
Towing service in Limburg starts from €125 per tow. I always discuss the price in advance, so that there are no surprises afterwards.
Can I get in touch via WhatsApp?
Yes. WhatsApp is often the fastest: send your location, vehicle and what happened, possibly with a photo.
Do you also do international transport?
Yes. In addition to local call-outs in Limburg, I also arrange vehicle transport outside Belgium, depending on destination and scheduling.
