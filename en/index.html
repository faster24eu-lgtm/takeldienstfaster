FAQ

Answers before you get in touch.

Practical information, no marketing waffle.

How quickly can you get to me?
That depends on location, traffic, how busy things are and urgency. For urgent call-outs, around 30 minutes is often possible.
Do I get a price upfront?
Yes. With your location, destination, make/model and situation, I can usually agree a price straight away.
When might a price change?
Only when the situation or distance given turns out to be clearly different from reality.
Can you take my car to a garage?
Yes. To a garage, home, port or another destination, including abroad.
Do you handle forklifts and machinery?
Yes. The right loading and lifting equipment matters for that.
Can you help at night?
Yes. Faster is reachable 24/7 via WhatsApp.
